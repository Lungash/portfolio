03 Running on

Bitaxe, solo

I solo mine Bitcoin on Bitaxe devices. A Bitaxe is a small open-source ASIC miner that sits on a desk. Solo means no pool.

The odds are long, and I know it. I do it anyway because it keeps me close to how the network really works: block templates, difficulty, the stratum protocol.
